# OpenDrone brand

## Identity

The OpenDrone wordmark is the primary identity. The OD mark is for avatars,
favicons and small spaces. Use the Incutec lockup when the company relationship
needs to be explicit.

## Colour

Canonical screen values live in `tokens/`. Physical supplier specifications
live in `standards/production.json`.

- `color.brand.gold` is the identity colour on every ground.
- Gold is not accessible text, focus or a control boundary on a light ground.
- Use the supplied light/dark artwork rather than adjusting colours.
- Never specify physical production from a screen value. Match the approved
  Pantone chip and production panel.

## Logo use

- Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, outline, add effects or rebuild the type.
- Keep clear space equal to at least the height of the O on every side.
- Minimum wordmark width is 120 px on screen or 25 mm in print. Use the mark
  below that size.
- Send vendors SVG or PDF, not PNG.

## Trademark

Editorial reference, reviews and compatibility statements are allowed. Ask
before merchandise, endorsement-like use or incorporating the identity into
another product name or logo. The hardware licence does not grant trademark
rights.
